stereogram

september 2026 · three lenses, one frame

Building a stereo adapter for my camera, and some custom software to animate the images.

the camera

First buy a disposable camera. Make sure to take photos with your friends!

Next take the camera apart for its lenses. Use blunt force, they don't make these things easy access.

Get the 3D printed adapter made. I had this made many years ago and had been holding onto it all this time. I don't know where I got the files, but they exist online so look them up.

the image(s)

The camera will project 3 images onto the frame, all from the different perspectives of the lenses. These are kind of already cool on their own.

wiggles

I (had Claude) put together some software to cut the images up and animate them. Make sure to orient each of the images around an origin point, like a flower that can be seen in all three perspectives. Otherwise the wiggles won't line up if the adapter isn't perfectly horizontal.
